  The general-purpose AI provisions of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 became applicable,
  imposing documentation, copyright-policy and training-data-summary duties on providers
  of GPAI models placed on the EU market, with additional obligations for models deemed
  to carry systemic risk.

Worth noting for readers of this archive: "GPAI" in the Regulation is a **legal** category
with thresholds attached, not the capability description used elsewhere on this site. The
two overlap but are not interchangeable, and conflating them makes both arguments worse.
See [general-purpose AI](/wiki/general-purpose-ai/) for the distinction.
