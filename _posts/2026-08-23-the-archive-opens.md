The archive is live with its first entries: four papers, two roadmaps, one implementation,
six concepts and three open problems. Everything here is a Markdown file in a public
repository, and the pull request queue is open.

## What goes where

The [contribution guide](/contribute/) has the full schema for each section. In short:
`/papers/` is for annotated research entries, `/roadmaps/` for signed and falsifiable
proposals, `/systems/` for code a stranger can run, `/wiki/` for short neutral definitions,
`/open-problems/` for what nobody has solved, and `/pages/` for your own work in your own
voice.

## On the seed entries

The initial papers, roadmaps and implementation are well-known published work, summarised
here in the archive's voice. They exist to establish the house style and to give the
cross-linking something to point at. They are not a claim about what is most important —
that is what the pull request queue is for.
