## Why it is contested rather than merely open

Some hold that this is unsolvable in principle — that Goodhart's law applies to any
published measure, and that the correct response is a rotating private evaluation regime
rather than a better metric. Others hold that a measure defined over *task-acquisition
cost* rather than task performance would be robust, because optimising against it is
indistinguishable from genuinely improving.

The archive files this as contested because both positions have serious proponents and the
disagreement is substantive, not terminological.

## What would count as progress

- A measure with a stated invariance property, plus evidence it survives direct optimisation.
- A formal argument that no such measure exists, which would settle it the other way.
