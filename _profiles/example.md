This is what a contributor page looks like. Copy `_profiles/example.md` to
`_profiles/your-handle.md`, replace the front matter, and write whatever you want here.

The strip above marks the page as yours and states that the views are your own. That frame
is what lets this namespace be permissive while the archive sections stay neutral.

## What people use these for

- A statement of what you are working on and why.
- A research agenda too speculative for `/roadmaps/`, or too personal for it.
- An index of your own entries elsewhere in the archive.
- Open questions you would like collaborators on.

## What is still enforced

Accurate attribution, no misrepresentation of others' work, and no content that would be
unlawful to publish. Beyond that, the page is yours.

## Everything the archive supports works here

Mathematics renders: $$\nabla_\theta \mathcal{L} = \E_{x}[\nabla_\theta \log p_\theta(x)]$$.
So do code blocks, tables, figures, video and audio — see the
[contribution guide](/contribute/) for the full set of includes.
